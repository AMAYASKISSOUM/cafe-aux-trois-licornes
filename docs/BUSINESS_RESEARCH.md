### Business Concept / Description

The business is a **café that doubles as a board-game space**. This is not a side detail — it is the core concept:

- Website header tagline: **"Café espace ludique"** (playful/game-space café).
- The official site's own booking system sells **reservable game tables** under the name **"Expérience licorne (Jeux, Café & Bouffe)"** ("Unicorn Experience — Games, Coffee & Food"), bookable in 30-minute, 60-minute, or 2-hour blocks, at no charge, with a note that seating is limited and reservations are recommended. Source: [reservation page](https://www.cafetroislicornes.com/s/appointments).
- Instagram bio: **"Le Café Aux Trois Licornes, le café le plus magique et le plus unique de tout l'Outaouais"** ("...the most magical and most unique café in all of Outaouais"). Source: [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/cafeauxtroislicornes/).
- Google reviewer keyword tags corroborate this: "jeux de société" (board games, tagged 3×), "piano" (3×), "train" (a toy/model train that entertains children, tagged 6×). Source: Google Maps review tag cloud.

No single formal "Google Business description paragraph" field was exposed in the publicly accessible (non-logged-in) Maps view — the concept description above is reconstructed from the tagline, booking system, and social bio, all owner-authored.

### Parking

### Business History / Story / Ownership

- **Founder/owner: Matthieu Maurice**, who also operates under the personal culinary brand **"L'Imposteur Culinaire"** (imposteurculinaire.com). Source: [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/cafeauxtroislicornes/) (account name), cross-referenced with [imposteurculinaire.com](https://www.imposteurculinaire.com).
- **Founding/opening date:** Facebook lists the page's "date de fondation" (founding date) as **June 1, 2025**. A pre-opening Instagram announcement post from the same era states an opening date of **June 3**. (Minor date discrepancy — see Section 3.) This makes the café roughly 14–15 months old as of this research (2026-08-30).
- **Origin story** (paraphrased from the founder's own Instagram announcement post, not quoted at length for copyright reasons): the founder describes naming the café after his three children — poetically called "three unicorns," each different yet alike — as a personal legacy, rather than naming it after himself. He describes it as his first permanent physical business location ("mon tout premier vrai pied-à-terre"), co-parenting with a partner named "Isa." He set an opening-week countdown with the tagline *"le café le plus magique et le plus unique de tout l'Outaouais."* Source: [Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJeILPDRNPR/).
- **Wider business context** (secondary source — the founder's own separate site, not the café's own materials, so treat as supplementary): [imposteurculinaire.com](https://www.imposteurculinaire.com) describes "Matt" as a self-taught, self-branded chef ("not a Chef, but an IMPOSTOR") who pivoted into cooking professionally around January 2020, and who — in addition to the café — has run a food truck (Cantley) and an event restaurant (Gatineau), plus catering, at-home chef services, meal boxes, and published (keto-focused) cookbooks. That site lists Martin Deshaies (Agence Tram) as manager and Gabrielle Cormier Béique as assistant — this appears to relate to the broader "Imposteur Culinaire" business rather than café-specific staffing, and should be confirmed before use.
- Reviewers repeatedly confirm the owner is personally present and hands-on: *"le propriétaire est très sympathique"* (K.D.), *"Proprio toujours sur place quand j'y vais et il aide même à monter la poussette"* ("owner always on-site when I visit, even helps carry the stroller up" — Annabel Brunet-Beaudry), and the owner personally replies to Google reviews (see Parking, above).
